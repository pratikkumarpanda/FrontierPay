9   INSTALLATION — why there is no flat rate: Installation/assembly payments are not a standalone treaty withholding category in any of these 5 treaties. Treatment depends on (1) whether technical know-how is transferred alongside the installation — if so, it is typically re-characterized as FTS and taxed at the FTS rate above; or (2) whether the installation activity itself creates a Permanent Establishment (most treaties have an "installation PE" duration threshold, commonly around 6 months/183 days, but the EXACT threshold differs by treaty and has not been verified per-country in this build). If no PE is created and no know-how transfer occurs, the payment is generally business profits taxable ONLY in the vendor's home country (0% Indian withholding). DO NOT hardcode a percentage for Installation without this classification step — it is the single most misclassification-prone category in the entire table, and treating it as a flat rate would be a real, checkable error.
